## Where these come from

The excerpts are drawn from a real first-draft manuscript in Brazilian Portuguese, with the author's permission — the system's first field test, which exercised the revision cycle (`analyze-chapter` → `R:` → rewrite → `review-revision`). The generation and knowledge layers have not yet been tested at book length. The analysis commentary follows the system convention: written in the project's output language — here, Portuguese, since the manuscript is Portuguese.

**Density figures in the sample are illustrative.** That session predated the per-1k unit; the numbers shown have the right shape but are not measurements. See the note at the top of the sample file.

They were chosen because they demonstrate the three-way interplay that makes the system work:

1. The analysis **quotes literally and suggests concretely**
2. The author **decides item by item** — accepts, alters, rejects, or asks back
3. Rejections become **signatures**; questions get **answers**; the system learns

Note especially the items where the author pushed back ("é uma marca minha" — *it's my signature*) — those decisions flowed into `persona.md` and stopped being flagged in every subsequent analysis. That feedback loop, not the analysis itself, is the product.
