# Template — Character Sheet

One sheet per character, in `.project/knowledge/characters/{name}.md`.

The goal is not to fill everything in. It is to record **what matters for writing** this character consistently.

> **Write character sheets in the project's output language.**

---

```markdown
# {Name or designation}

_Last updated: {date}_

## Identity

**Name:** {or "unnamed — designated as X"}
**Narrative function:** {protagonist, antagonist, catalyst, chorus, mirror...}
**Appears in:** {chapters}

## Body and presence

{How they occupy space. What the reader notices first. Details that recur.}

## Voice

**Register:** {elevated, colloquial, monosyllabic, verbose...}
**Own vocabulary:** {words only they use}
**Speech rhythm:** {short sentences? long? hesitations?}
**What they never say:** {taboos, avoidances}

**Sample line:**
> {literal quote from the manuscript}

## Interior

**Wants:** {conscious desire, what they pursue}
**Needs:** {real need, often opposed to the want}
**Fears:** {what drives them underneath}
**Lies about:** {what they hide, from themselves or others}

## Arc

**Starting state:** {where they begin}
**Turning point:** {what changes and when}
**Ending state:** {where they land}

## Relationships

- **{other character}** — {nature of the relationship, what shifts across the book}

## Consistency rules

{Things that must always hold true. Established facts that cannot be contradicted.}

- {fact}
- {fact}

## Writing notes

{Reminders for when writing scenes with this character. Traps to avoid.}

## Signature lines

{Lines or constructions belonging to this character. Cross-reference preserve-list.md.}

- "{line}"
```

---

## Worked example (fragment)

```markdown
# The innkeeper

**Narrative function:** chorus / social thermometer of the village
**Appears in:** 01.01, 01.02, 02.01, 02.03, 04.01

## Voice

**Register:** monosyllabic by design. Extreme verbal economy.
**Rhythm:** rarely exceeds three words.
**What they never say:** anything self-incriminating. Never asks personal questions.

**Sample line:**
> — Tem cama.

## Consistency rules

- Never leaves the counter on-page.
- Communicates by chin gestures and grunts.
- The quality of his food improves as the protagonist is absorbed into the village — this is an indicator, not coincidence.

## Writing notes

Careful with the tic of over-translating his grunts. The economy *is* the characterization; explaining too much destroys the effect.
```

---

## Notes

**Not every sheet needs every section.** A chorus character needs voice and consistency rules. A protagonist needs everything.

**"Consistency rules" is the most useful section** for `check-consistency` and for avoiding contradictions across chapters.

**Always use literal quotes.** The Voice section only works with real examples from the manuscript.
