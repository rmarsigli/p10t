# References — {Book title}

_Version 0 — to fill_

Authors and works informing the voice of **this specific book**. Not a favourites list — a working tool.

The operative question is not "who do you like?" but **"what exactly do you want to borrow?"**.

> **Write this file in the project's output language.**

---

## How to fill it in

One sheet per reference. Be specific: "Murakami" does not help generate prose. "Murakami: the way the mundane is described with the same attention as the supernatural, with no shift in register" does.

---

## Sheet template

```markdown
### {Author} — {Specific work, if applicable}

**What to take:**
- {concrete, imitable aspect}
- {another aspect}

**What NOT to take:**
- {aspect that does not fit this book}

**Example of what I want:**
> {quote from the reference author, if available}

**How this shows up in my book:**
{where you already did this, or where you intend to}
```

---

## Worked example

```markdown
### Kazuo Ishiguro — The Remains of the Day

**What to take:**
- An unreliable narrator who does not know he is unreliable
- Emotion surfacing through what the narrator *avoids* saying
- Long, formal sentence rhythm as characterization, not ornament

**What NOT to take:**
- The total restraint. My narrator has more humour and more venting.

**How this shows up in my book:**
Act 2, when the investigator describes village routine without realizing he is describing his own absorption.
```
