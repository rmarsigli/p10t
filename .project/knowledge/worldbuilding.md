## Rules

```markdown
### {rule name}
**Makes possible:** {the capability or phenomenon}
**Costs:** {price, limit, side effect}
**Known by:** {who understands it, who is wrong about it}
**Status:** {ESTABLISHED — stated on the page | ASSUMED — implied, still changeable}
```

## Worked example

```markdown
## Premise
The world is being unmade, slowly, and nobody remembers what has already gone.

## Rules

### Erasure
**Makes possible:** removal of people, structures, and memory from the world
**Costs:** nobody notices — the memory of the erased thing goes with it
**Known by:** nobody in-world; the merchant suspects; the notebook records what the mind cannot
**Status:** ESTABLISHED
**Source:** 01.03 (the red door), 02.01 (the missing house)

### The notebook
**Makes possible:** written record survives erasure longer than memory does
**Costs:** not indefinitely — pages blank out eventually; ink fades before paper
**Known by:** the investigator, by accident
**Status:** ESTABLISHED
**Source:** 01.02

## Deliberately unexplained
- **Why the cycle exists at all** — the reader should feel the weight of repetition without a cosmology to argue with. Explaining it would make it a system instead of a condition.
```
