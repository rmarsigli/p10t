_Story time: {when this happens, per timeline.md}_

{The author's one-line intent for this chapter.}

## Obligations

### Debt (pays)

### Debt (deliberately deferred)
- {open promise} → {rides until ch. Y}

### Planting
- {what future chapter Z needs seeded here, and how invisibly}

### Protection (must NOT happen)
- {mystery that stays closed / info a character cannot yet have / reveal reserved for later}

## Scenes

### Scene 1 — {label}
**Purpose:** {the one obligation this serves}
**Conflict:** {what resists}
**Turn:** {what is different at the end}
**On stage:** {characters — sheets to load}
**Length:** {brief | standard | extended}

### Scene 2 — {label}
...

## Ending

**Pull:** {what makes the reader start the next chapter}
**Closing pattern:** {and what it should NOT be, per recent chapters' endings}

## Open questions

{What the author has not decided yet. Drafting stops at these boundaries.}
